The Fine Print

Privacy

Short version: this site sets no cookies and runs no tracking.

What this site collects

Nothing, today. Wuhen & Xuan: Heaven Defying is a static promotional website. It uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking pixels, and has no accounts or forms. We do not collect, store, or sell any personal data.

If we later enable analytics, it will be a privacy-friendly, cookie-free counter (GoatCounter), and this page will be updated first.

Third parties involved in serving the site

Like almost any website, some infrastructure sees your connection when pages load:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site and, like any web host, may log standard request data (such as IP addresses) for security and operational purposes, under GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the two typefaces; your browser requests the font files from Google's servers, which see your IP address, per the Google Fonts privacy FAQ.

We never see or store any of that data ourselves.

If you email us

If you write to zhekov.yane123@gmail.com (for example to be notified about the demo), we receive your email address and whatever you send. It is used only to reply and to send you the update you asked for — never shared or sold. Ask at any time and it will be deleted.

Your rights (GDPR)

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, correct, and erase personal data held about you, and to object to or restrict its processing. Since the only personal data we could hold is an email you chose to send us, exercising these rights is one message away: zhekov.yane123@gmail.com.

Changes

Any change to what this site collects (such as enabling analytics or adding a newsletter) will be reflected on this page before it goes live.
