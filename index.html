Questions from the Mortal Realm

Frequently Asked

What is Wuhen & Xuan: Heaven Defying?

A dark xianxia (cultivation) action-RPG. You survive a village massacre, unlock a hidden bloodline, and uncover the political conspiracy behind the attack.

What platforms will it be on?

PC (Windows) first. Other platforms may be considered after the PC release.

When does the demo release?

The demo — a roughly 20-minute vertical slice — is still in development. There is no release date yet; it will be announced the moment one exists.

How can I follow development?

Use the Get Notified button above, or email zhekov.yane123@gmail.com — you'll hear from us when the demo is playable.
