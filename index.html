01 / AN ORDINARY LIFE

Nothing lasts. Not even peace.

He walks away from the village, toward the trees. Then the ground shakes. He turns back.

His village. In flames.

He runs home. His uncle is still inside.

“Don’t avenge me.”

“Forget about me… Live a good life. A long life.”

Avenge…? Those were monsters. Unless someone sent them.

A life, recorded in ink.

Six years pass. Strength. Survival. Discipline. He learns to live alone.

Some paths find you.

He finds a scroll in the forest and opens it. His story has only begun.
