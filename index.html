01 / AN ORDINARY MORNING

Nothing ever happened here.

Jiang Wuhen walks the street of Cinderfall Hamlet with a basket for mushrooms. His uncle waves from the herb shop. Neighbours call his name. At the gate, the garrison is marching out early. No one asks why.

02 / THE SKY TURNS

Then the forest held its breath.

At the edge of the old pines, picking mushrooms, he notices the forest has gone quiet. Over the valley, clouds pour out of a clear sky. Gold and violet light flashes inside them: something above the clouds is at war. A streak of light falls beyond the western ridge. Day turns to night in a single breath.

03 / CINDERFALL BURNS

Behind him, the village is burning.

Ashen wolves run along the rooftops and lightning strikes the road. He drops the basket and runs home through the same street he walked that morning. The guard post is empty. The door of his uncle’s house is already open.

04 / A FINAL WARNING

“Don’t avenge me.”

His uncle lies under a fallen beam. “Forget this place. Forget me. Live a good life. A long one.” His hand falls, and a golden light rises out of him, up through the broken roof, over the burning village. Avenge…? Those were monsters. Unless someone sent them. On the crag above the village, figures in black masks watch Cinderfall burn beneath violet banners.

05 / THE YEARS BETWEEN

The Forbidden Forest.

He follows the light west until the fires are only a glow behind him, over the ridges and into the forest no one enters. The first winter nearly kills him. He learns snares, tracks and the bow. Every winter he measures himself against a pale tree and cuts a mark in the bark. Six years.

06 / WHAT FELL THAT NIGHT

Some paths find you.

In the sixth autumn there is no game near the camp, not one track, so he goes farther than he ever has. The forest grows older and the air begins to glow: motes of light drifting, then streaming, all toward a hollow under the oldest tree. There, sealed in crystal by the years, waits what fell from the sky the night Cinderfall burned: a scroll. His story has only begun.
